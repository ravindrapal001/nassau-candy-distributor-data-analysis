# Day 4 — Excel Business Profiling and PivotTables

## Project
**Nassau Candy Distributor — Shipping Route Efficiency Analysis**

## Objective
Perform business profiling and exploratory data analysis (EDA) in Microsoft Excel using PivotTables and charts to analyze shipping modes, regional performance, product divisions, and state-level sales.

## Dataset
- **Source:** Nassau Candy Distributor dataset
- **Previously reported records:** 10,194
- **Previously reported attributes:** 18

*Note: These figures and all analytical results must be verified against the current source dataset.*

## Tasks

### 1. Ship Mode Analysis
- Group records by Ship Mode.
- Calculate record count, total Sales, Units, Gross Profit, and Cost.
- Calculate distinct Order ID counts when supported.
- Create a Sales chart and a separate record-count chart.

### 2. Regional Performance
- Group records by Region.
- Calculate record count, total Sales, Units, Gross Profit, and Cost.
- Create Sales by Region and Gross Profit by Region charts.

### 3. Product Division Analysis
- Group records by Division.
- Calculate record count, total Sales, Gross Profit, and Units.

### 4. State-Level Business Profile
- Group records by State/Province.
- Calculate record count, total Sales, and Gross Profit.
- Sort by Sales in descending order.
- Filter the Top 10 states by Sales and create a chart.

## Excel Techniques
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Aggregation using Sum and Count
- Distinct Count where available
- Sorting and Top 10 value filters
- Number formatting and chart design
- Reconciliation against validated source totals

## Validation Rules
- Preserve the original dataset.
- Distinguish record counts from distinct order counts.
- Do not treat repeated Order IDs as duplicate records automatically.
- Verify numerical totals against the source data.
- Investigate date anomalies before making shipping-efficiency claims.
- Do not report unverified results or unsupported business conclusions.

## Deliverables
- Excel workbook containing the Day 4 business analysis.
- Four PivotTables.
- Relevant charts.
- Validation evidence and documented limitations.
